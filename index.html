Founder & Artistic Director

Charanya Gurusathya

A Bharatanatyam artist, teacher, choreographer and researcher, Charanya founded Natyashala to keep traditional training precise, expressive and deeply human.

  • 1Dual academic background with MBA and Masters in Fine Arts in Bharatanatyam; pursuing doctoral research in dance.
  • 2Delegate speaker at the International Dance Council General Assembly at UNESCO Headquarters, Paris, with workshops and performances across India, USA, France and Germany.
  • 3Published dance research including work on dance as a stress-busting practice, connecting classical art with wellbeing and education.
  • 4Guides students through rigorous learning, stage productions, arangetrams and the academy's annual ABHYASA Pradarshanam.

Training Journey

From first class to confident stage presence.

The academy is structured for steady progress. Every learner receives patient correction, performance exposure and a clear path forward.

Foundation

Posture, rhythm, adavus, body alignment, vocabulary, discipline and joy in practice.

Expression

Abhinaya, mythology, musical listening, hand gestures, facial expression and storytelling.

Performance

Stage etiquette, costume preparation, ensemble rehearsals, solo confidence and live orchestra awareness.

Arangetram

Long-form preparation for the solo debut, with repertoire maturity and rigorous rehearsal cycles.
