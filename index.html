Our Story

A Legacy of Classical Excellence

Rooted in tradition, blossoming through dedicated practice

Guided by Charanya Gurusathya

Natyashala Academy of Fine Arts is a Pune-based centre for training, performing, and research in classical dance. Founded and led by Charanya Gurusathya — a distinguished Bharatanatyam artist, PhD Scholar, and educator with over 17 years of dance excellence.

Charanya has presented her research at international seminars including MSU Baroda, received recognition from UNESCO Vice President Prof. Constantin Kontogiannis, conducted workshops in Paris with the Triwat Dance School, and performed as a jury member in Italy. Her annual showcase ABHYASA Pradarshanam is a landmark event in Pune's cultural calendar.

What We Teach

Our Programs

Classical arts handed down through generations, taught with devotion

💃

Bharatanatyam

One of India's oldest classical dance forms, originating from Tamil Nadu. Learn the precise Nritta (pure dance), Nritya (expressional dance), and Natya (drama) components.

Beginner Intermediate Advanced
🕺

Kathak

The lyrical dance of North India, known for its swift spins, intricate footwork, and expressive storytelling. Master the grace of both Lucknow and Jaipur gharanas.

Beginner Intermediate Advanced
🎵

Carnatic Music

The classical music of South India — rooted in devotion and raga intricacy. Vocal training covering Swarajathi, Varnam, Kriti compositions and ragas.

Vocal Theory All Ages
🎶

Hindustani Music

The classical music tradition of North India with rich raga elaborations and Khayal, Thumri, and Bhajan. Develop a deep connection with melody and emotion.

Vocal Ragas All Ages
🌸

Indian Folk Dance

A vibrant series celebrating the diverse folk traditions of India — Lavani (Maharashtra), Ghoomar (Rajasthan), Kummi Kollattam & Karagam (Tamil Nadu), Pahadi (Himachal Pradesh), and Gondhal (Maharashtra).

Lavani Ghoomar Kummi Kollattam
